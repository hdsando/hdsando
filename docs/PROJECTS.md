# Project portfolio

[Back to profile](../README.md) · [Research and professional background](PROFILE.md)

These projects bring together my work on audit, finance, learning and digital products. The entries distinguish public sources, private development and projects at the scoping stage.

## S.A.F.A.

**Financial audit automation | Public sources**

S.A.F.A. is an Excel/VBA project for financial audit workflows. Its source modules cover data ingestion, reconciliation, statistical anomaly analysis and reporting.

[Project overview](SAFA.md) · [Source modules](../src/vba/) · [Validation plan](VALIDATION.md)

The repository contains sources and documentation. A packaged workbook, reproducible demonstration and benchmark remain to be published.

## FINLIFE

**Personal finance | Private development**

A personal finance application designed for African users, bringing together budgeting, financial habits and the organisation of everyday finances.

The application is in development. Its repository remains private; this entry presents its general purpose.

## KILIMA

**Financial inclusion and applied research | Private prototype**

A project exploring financial inclusion and explainable decision support, with an emphasis on traceability and human judgement.

The work is at the prototype stage. This entry describes the research direction rather than a finished financial service.

## illimna

**Learning and project management | Private development**

A French-language learning platform for project management exam preparation, combining courses, interactive exercises, case studies and practice exams.

The project is in development. Its source code and internal learning materials remain private.

## Siéra Studio

**Graphic design and digital presence | Private development**

A website presenting a graphic design studio's services, offers, brand identity and portfolio. The project connects visual communication with an accessible digital presentation of the studio's work.

The source repository remains private. This entry provides a general description of the project.

## Proactive GRC

**Governance, risk and compliance | Initial scoping**

A public workspace for a governance, risk and compliance project. At this stage, the repository provides an initial project brief and a proposed roadmap; there is no application implementation.

[Repository and project brief](https://github.com/hdsando/proactive-grc)
